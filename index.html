FAQ

Good questions.

Is G-Stocks a broker or a bank?

No. G-Stocks is the onboarding, money and tax layer. A SEBI-registered broker approves your KYC and executes your orders, and an Authorised Dealer bank holds your money. We never hold your funds or shares.

Who can invest?

Non-Resident Indians, Overseas Citizens of India and foreign nationals. Since June 2026, FEMA and RBI rules allow any individual resident outside India to buy listed Indian shares.

Is there a limit on how much I can own?

Yes. Overseas individuals can hold up to 10% of a company, and 24% in aggregate, after the August 2026 increase. We show these limits when you build an order.

How long does it take?

Doing it yourself from the US takes about 8–14 weeks to the first trade. Our target is 3–5 weeks. The final figure depends on SEBI's remote-KYC rule, which is still a proposal.

What does it cost?

We target a one-time $79 concierge fee, plus partner setup costs of about $100–200. Brokerage and statutory charges are the same as anywhere else. We do not take a share of brokerage or FX margin.

Do you give investment advice?

No. We provide education and tools. You choose your investments and confirm every order at your broker.

What about taxes?

We prepare India and US tax reports for you to file, including the India ITR, FBAR and Form 8938 inputs. Please confirm your filing with a qualified CA or CPA.

Is G-Stocks live yet?

Not yet. We are pre-launch and building with partners. Join the early-access list below and we will write to you.
